Our robotic car is designed and built to autonomously navigate unknown areas and create accurate 2D/3D maps. Also, the vehicles are capable of intra-vehicular and vehicle-to-infrastructure communication. In this documentation, we go through the hardware design, power setup, and initial boot-up, followed by software installation, driving calibration, and course curriculum.

> [!Warning] 
> Running ROS 1?
> Please see our deprecated ROS 1 Documentation [here](https://airou-lab.github.io/docs/intro.html).
